How to embed on your website

Paste these two lines before the closing </body> tag on your website:

<script>
  window.HNConfig = {
    apiUrl: "https://YOUR-DEPLOYED-SERVER.com/chat",
    botName: "Himalayan Naturals",
    accentColor: "#7c4a85"
  };
</script>
<script src="https://YOUR-DEPLOYED-SERVER.com/chatbot.js"></script>

Customisation options

  • apiUrl — your deployed server URL + /chat
  • botName — name shown in the chat header
  • accentColor — hex colour for the bubble and messages
