# Himalyan Naturals — Knowledge Base

## BRAND

Himalyan Naturals is a hormone-conscious personal care and functional botanical brand founded by Harmanjeet Rekhi.
Our products are designed to support women navigating hormonal transitions, including perimenopause, menopause, PCOS, endometriosis, and lifestyle-driven hormonal imbalance.

We follow an **Endo Neutral philosophy**, which means our formulations are designed to avoid unnecessary interference with the body’s hormonal system.

We believe in evidence-informed, plant-based support rooted in Ayurveda, ecological intelligence, and modern understanding of endocrine health.
Every product is created using clean, forest-derived ingredients from Uttarakhand, India, under the Non-Timber Forest Products framework.

## BRAND PHILOSOPHY AND VALUES

We follow the principle of **Endo Neutral living**.

This means that unless there is a clear therapeutic intention, products that enter or contact the body should not interfere with hormonal function.

We take a long-view approach to women's health.
We do not believe in masking symptoms.
We believe in reducing hormonal noise and supporting the body’s natural regulatory intelligence.

Our ingredients are sourced from forests of Uttarakhand under the Non-Timber Forest Products framework, ensuring ecological balance and ingredient integrity.

We combine Ayurvedic wisdom with modern understanding of endocrine health.

We believe in **Conscious Ayurveda** —
not blind natural living, but informed, aware, and intentional choices.
